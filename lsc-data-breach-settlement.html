Who qualifies

Your personal information was potentially compromised in the Laboratory Services Cooperative (LSC) data incident learned of on or about October 27, 2024 — including if you received or paid for lab tests ordered by a Planned Parenthood health center on or before that date.

A pro rata cash payment of up to $1,000 on the no-documentation tier; documented losses can be claimed at a higher tier with proof.
