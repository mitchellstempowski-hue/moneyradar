Apple Siri / Apple Intelligence Settlement

$250 million settlement fund · Claim deadline December 21, 2026 ·

Who qualifies

You bought an iPhone 15 Pro, iPhone 15 Pro Max, or any iPhone 16 model (including 16e, Plus, Pro, and Pro Max) in the United States between June 10, 2024 and March 29, 2025. The case (Landsheft v. Apple) concerns Siri and Apple Intelligence features that were advertised but did not arrive on schedule. Apple denies wrongdoing.

What it pays

A presumptive $25 per eligible device, adjustable up or down depending on how many people file, capped at $95.

Proof: No receipt — your device serial number stands in for proof of purchase.

3. Save your confirmation.
