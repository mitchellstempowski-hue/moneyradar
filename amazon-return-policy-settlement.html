Amazon Return Policy Settlement

$309.5 million settlement fund · Most members are paid automatically · Members re-charged after a refund must claim by December 1, 2026 ·

Who qualifies

You are a U.S. customer who initiated a return or refund for a physical product bought on Amazon.com and received in the U.S. between September 5, 2017 and February 12, 2026 — and you either never got your refund, got it late, or got the wrong amount (Subclass A), or you got a refund and were then incorrectly re-charged for the item (Subclass B). The case is In re Amazon Return Policy Litigation.

What it pays

The fund is $309.5 million and non-reversionary — it does not go back to Amazon. Subclass A members are paid automatically from 92.7% of the net fund, with no claim form needed. Subclass B members share 7.3% of the net fund but must file a claim by December 1, 2026. Individual amounts have not been published and will depend on the administrator's records and how many valid claims are filed — nobody can promise you a specific number. The final approval hearing is March 16, 2027.

How to file — free

1. Go to the official site: returnsettlement.com
2. If you were re-charged after a refund (Subclass B), complete the claim form before December 1, 2026.
3. Save your confirmation.
If you are in Subclass A, no action is needed — payment is automatic if the settlement is finally approved.
