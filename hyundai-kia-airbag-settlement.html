Hyundai and Kia Airbag Control Unit Settlement

$62.1 million in payments and credits · Claim deadline April 8, 2027 ·

Who qualifies

You owned or leased, as of April 14, 2025, a covered Hyundai or Kia vehicle originally sold or leased in the U.S. that contains a ZF-TRW airbag control unit (ACU). The case is In re ZF-TRW ACU Products Liability. Eligibility is decided by your vehicle's VIN — the official site has the lookup.

What it pays

The settlement provides $62,100,100 in payments and credits. Residual payments are up to $350 for recalled vehicles and up to $150 for unrecalled vehicles, plus reimbursement of recall-related expenses and a 10-year warranty on new parts. Exact amounts depend on how many valid claims are filed — nobody can promise you a specific number. Final approval was already granted on October 8, 2025.

Proof: Eligibility is VIN-based — have your VIN ready.

How to file — free

1. Go to the official site: acusettlement.com/hyundaikia
2. Check your VIN and complete the claim form before April 8, 2027.
3. Save your confirmation.
The claim form asks you to certify under penalty of perjury that you belong to the class.
