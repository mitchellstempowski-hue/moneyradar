Columbia University Data Breach Settlement

$16.125 million settlement fund · Claim deadline January 21, 2027 ·

Who qualifies

You are a U.S. resident whose private information was compromised in the cyberattack on Columbia University discovered in May 2025 — and Columbia sent you a notice about it. Membership in this class comes from the university's own records: if you were sent a notice, you are in the group the settlement covers.

What it pays

Up to $8,500 for documented losses, or an alternative cash payment if you'd rather not document losses — plus 2 years of free credit monitoring either way. The alternative payment amount is set after claims are counted, so nobody can promise you a specific number.

Proof: Your Notice ID is required to file; documentation is required only for the documented-losses tier.

How to file — free

1. Go to the official site: columbiauniversitydatasettlement.com
2. Enter the Notice ID from the notice Columbia sent you, and complete the claim form before January 21, 2027.
3. Save your confirmation.
The claim form asks you to certify under penalty of perjury that you belong to the class.
